## What remains uncertain

Missing paid/public transcripts, historical publication vintages, complete portfolio instructions and some physical-event sources remain unavailable. Macro samples are small, dependent and vulnerable to specification choice. US historical evidence has not validated a global political-risk score. A prospective tool has no demonstrated decision benefit or investment-performance record yet.

These limitations are retained beside their consequential claims in the main report. They do not warrant reversing unsupported conclusions into equally unsupported assertions that the commentators are useless, dishonest, incapable of ever predicting events, or guaranteed to improve financial outcomes.
