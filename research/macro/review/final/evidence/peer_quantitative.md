## Recommended wording refinements

1. In the joint-pool paragraph, name **Baa minus Aaa** explicitly instead of “the corporate-spread rank.” Elsewhere the report uses Baa–Treasury and HY measures; their percentile histories cannot inherit these counts.
2. In the profit paragraph, name the 4.17 and 1.58 pp errors **root-mean-square errors (RMSE)**. The current generic “error” is numerically correct but underspecified.

An optional useful addition is that the matched expanding screens have positive paired energy-minus-market five-year medians (+8.62 / +6.62 pp), while the review screen's paired mean is −1.00 pp. That distinction reinforces the report's existing benchmark/sample-sensitivity conclusion; it is not required to make the report accurate.
