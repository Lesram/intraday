## Integrity and availability

[The pre-finalization manifest](../artifacts/source_integrity_before.json) fingerprints 1,200 existing research files outside dependencies, caches and this final directory, plus 14 pre-existing modified tracked files. Final validation compares those bytes again. Earlier 75-file blind and five-file second-reader seals and the prediction lock remain historical evidence. Consolidation 003's final seal records its 17 main deliverables/evidence files. Hashes establish byte identity, not factual truth.

The original source package and the full consolidation working directory are local companions. Published final CSV/JSON outputs and methodology are inspectable, while full regeneration requires the stated companion inputs. Public-file link checks distinguish deliberately local companion references from missing deliverables.

No new market-history download was used to silently move the research cutoff. Primary-source verification and methodological corrections are dated separately from the supplied market observations. A future acquisition process must store observations, release timestamps, retrieval timestamps, revisions and checksums independently.
