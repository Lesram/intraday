## Confirmed correction of the verifier

V6 calls Jikh's $849bn/42% claim a tripling of the Chicago Fed's14%. The primary paper says $849bn, or14%, **on life insurers' balance sheets in2024**. That denominator is insurer assets, not the private-credit market. Neither percentage can substitute for the other. $849bn divided by a separately chosen $2tn market produces42.45%, but that calculation also requires matched definitions: the paper's private placements include financial borrowers and privately placed ABS. Thus the speaker's market-share claim still needs a consistent market denominator; the prior correction is demonstrably wrong. Source checked2026-09-20: [Chicago Fed WP2025-09](https://www.chicagofed.org/publications/working-papers/2025/2025-09), revised2026-04-27. The earlier2025 PDF abstract uses the same denominator.

The BIS confirms that $14.2tn is outstanding yen-related FX swaps/forwards/currency swaps at end2023 and is mostly hedging/liquidity activity. It does **not** establish an identified $0.9–4.5tn carry-trade confidence range. Those narrower stocks are different proxies, also not all carry. “Gross notional is not speculative exposure” survives; the replacement interval and the blanket double-counted-legs assertion require independent support. Source checked2026-09-20: [BIS, Sizing up carry trades](https://www.bis.org/publications/sizing-carry-trades-bis-statistics), September2024.

## V4: numerical and temporal reasoning defects

* A documented oil spread **greater than25** or **greater than30** is a lower bound, not an upper bound. It cannot establish that35 is inflated by15–40%. Contemporaneous matched physical/futures quotes are required. An EIA spot series alone cannot verify a futures quote; benchmark and timestamp matter.
* The supply-gap list mixes overlapping offsets: China demand destruction is part of global demand changes, SPR releases may be included in inventory draws, and export rerouting is not necessarily new global production. It must not be added mechanically as independent supply.
* An eventual June cumulative loss above1.3bn barrels does not establish780m lost by April20. Need the contemporaneous interval integration.
* 287m barrels divided by1.4m barrels/day is205days, not “sustainable for years.” Initial413m gives295days. This arithmetic does not validate an80day exhaustion prediction, but it corrects the verifier's reassurance.
* Food prices falling by July does not refute the state of food prices described on May15. The source distinguishes price-level verification from future forecasts inconsistently.
* A later recorded first ceasefire violation is not proof that earlier covert/noncompliance events could not have occurred. MOU legally in force, practical shipping disruption and speaker's political judgment need distinct fields.
* “Highest since2009” is not logically incompatible with a new record: an interval maximum can exceed the earlier benchmark. V6 uses that purported contradiction to reject an Oracle superlative without matched CDS data.

## V6: measurement and evidence strength

* Market-value Treasury holdings plus a single10y yield do not identify transactions. Unknown duration, bills versus bonds, currencies, custody, maturities and sector composition prevent the claim that the **entire**63.8bn decline is price. Need TIC transactions and valuation reconciliation. Also “holdings ROSE over Q1” contradicts its own1046.3→1039.8 figures (−6.5bn).
* July/August events cannot validate May26 claims about cumulative intervention as of May. Likewise September financial readings cannot establish July23 conditions without retrieving July values.
* A10y JGB yield cannot verify the30y level; broad BusEq returns cannot refute a software-only return claim; realised yield volatility is not MOVE. Label them proxies and leave precise claims unresolved.
* “Legally required” versus capital incentives, whole-platform Kinexys volume versus JPMD, whole-market opacity versus a firm's exact assets, and correct contract arithmetic versus an unverified position count receive MOSTLY_TRUE too readily. A true nearby fact is not sufficient to verify the claim's load-bearing noun, denominator or date.
* No observed private-credit manager failure does not negate losses, redemption restrictions or other stress. Conversely specific fund restrictions do not establish a systemwide insolvency. Correct aggregation cuts in both directions.

These are source/method adjudications, separate from the randomly selected72-row blind second-reader experiment. They must not be added to its disagreement numerator.
