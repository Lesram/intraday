    fix(brain): refuse to overwrite trained manifest with untrained state
    
    Adds a defensive guard at the top of BrainPersistence.save() that blocks
    writes when the existing manifest shows total_trades > 0 or ml_is_trained
    is True, but the incoming learner/signal_gen state is fresh/untrained
    (total_trades == 0 AND not _is_trained).
    
    This is the real backstop against the Apr 8 02:08 UTC brain wipe failure
    mode. Root cause investigation proved the wipe came through the full
    save() path with fresh learner/signal_gen arguments, producing a manifest
    with generation=0, total_trades=0, ml_is_trained=false while preserving
    the stale self._manifest["total_runs"] counter.
    
    The guard is bypassed by force=True (used by LiveEngine.force_save_brain
    for explicit admin recovery) and by a fresh BrainPersistence instance
    (self._manifest empty — nothing to protect yet).
    
    Logs ERROR on block instead of raising, to avoid crashing the scheduler
    tick if the guard fires during normal operation.
