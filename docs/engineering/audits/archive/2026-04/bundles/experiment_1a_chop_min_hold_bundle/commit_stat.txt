    experiment(exits): chop-regime minimum-hold gate for pyramid_cut [Exp 1A]
    
    In chop regime, suppress pyramid_cut exits (close_partial from the
    pyramider) until the trade has been held for at least 10 bars.
    
    Hypothesis: the Apr 7-10 baseline shows 24/32 trades exit via
    pyramid_cut, ALL losers (-$63.80 total), while 78% of entries go
    green (MFE > 0) and the 5 timeout exits (18-30 bars) are ALL
    winners (+$18.68). Premature cuts in chop destroy edge that would
    have been captured by holding. A 10-bar minimum hold gives breakout
    entries time to work in a range-bound market instead of getting cut
    on temporary adverse excursions.
    
    Scope:
    - Only affects pyramid_cut (close_partial) exits in chop regime
    - stop_loss, horizon_timeout, max_holding_period unchanged
    - Non-chop pyramid_cut behavior unchanged
    - No entry logic changes
    - No sizing changes
    
    Instrumentation: logs "Exp1A: pyramid_cut suppressed (chop min-hold)"
    with symbol, bars_held, regime, R-multiple, unrealized PnL, and the
    suppressed reason when a cut would have fired but is held back.
